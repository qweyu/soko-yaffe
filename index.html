How it works

Farmers

Post your produce with quantity, price, and your phone number. Update or remove it when sold.

Buyers

Search by product or district, then call or message the farmer on WhatsApp to agree on price and pick-up.

Payment

Pay by mobile money or cash on delivery. Meet in a public place and check goods before paying.

No smartphone?

Plan for an SMS or USSD option and a community agent at the ICT centre who posts listings for farmers.

Soko Yaffe is a community project. Always confirm quality and price before you pay.
